# Privacy statement: techforge-ndpa

Last updated: 6 October 2026. Publisher: The Tech Forge (dtechforge.com). Contact: info@dtechforge.com.

This statement covers the **techforge-ndpa** plugin for Claude: the `ndpa-qa`, `ndpa-audit` and `ndpa-docs` skills and their reference files.

## What the plugin is

A set of instructions and reference texts (the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the NDPC's General Application and Implementation Directive 2025) that Claude reads when you ask about Nigerian data protection law, audit something against it, or draft a compliance document. It contains no program that runs on its own, no server and no connector.

## What The Tech Forge receives

**Nothing.** The plugin does not send any data to The Tech Forge, and The Tech Forge has no server that it connects to. We do not see your questions, your files, the reports the plugin produces, or how you use it. We do not use analytics or tracking.

## What happens to your data when you use it

The plugin works inside your own Claude session:

- **What you share with Claude.** Your questions, and any files, code, policies or systems you point it at for an audit, are processed by Claude as part of your conversation. That processing is done by Anthropic under your agreement with Anthropic and Anthropic's own privacy policy, not by The Tech Forge.
- **What it writes.** Reports and draft documents are written only where you ask, usually a folder you choose on your own computer or in your Claude workspace.
- **What it looks up.** To check that an NDPC fee, registration tier or deadline is still current, Claude may look up public pages on the NDPC's website (ndpc.gov.ng). These lookups request public pages; the plugin does not send your files or personal data to the NDPC or anyone else.

## Personal data in what you audit

Material you audit, such as a codebase, a CCTV setup or an HR file, may contain personal data about other people. You remain responsible for that data, as its controller or processor under the Act. Share only what the task needs, and keep the reports, which describe weaknesses in the systems examined, as confidential as the material itself.

## Children

The plugin is a professional tool and is not intended for people under 18.

## Changes

If the plugin's behaviour changes in a way that affects this statement, for example if it starts using a connector or a server, we will update this statement and its date before releasing that version.
